Hey!

Most of you probably know me as a content creator, but I've been a software
developer for much longer than that!

The vast majority of my previous work was for private companies. I'm finally
changing that and branching out into open-source territory. Hoping to make our
creator landscape just a little bit better, in whatever ways I can!

It's just the beginning so far, so I haven't got much to show! But this is
what's very generally on my roadmap:
- OBS Plugins
  - Been learning _a lot_ about plugin development
  - 1x project in active development
  - 1x project in planning phases
- OBS Improvements & Fixes
  - Been diving into a few bugs & issues that I'm a little too passionate about
- Two very exciting secret (for now) projects

## Made with organic intelligence

For now, I'm still personally on the train of authoring my own code.

When I make something, I want to _know_ it. LLMs do not currently offer much
value to me in that respect, and come with host of issues other people can
explain better than I can. I'd rather author 2-3 great things I understand well
and can maintain, than 100 LLM-generated projects that are effectively
abandonware the moment "I" release them.
